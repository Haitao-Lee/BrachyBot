BrachyBot Intelligent Planning Platform

End-to-end AI workflow for ¹²⁵I seed implantation and brachytherapy planning: image segmentation, dose prediction, trajectory planning and report generation for pancreatic, prostate, liver, lung, and gynaecological cases.

  • Smart segmentation (CTV / OAR) nnU-Net + TotalSegmentator auto-delineate the tumor and organs at risk.
  • 3D Dose Prediction (DoseUNet) Sub-millimeter sliding-window inference aggregates per-seed dose in seconds.
  • AI trajectory and seed planning Rule-based and reinforcement-learning hybrid generates OAR-aware safe trajectories.
  • Multi-agent live review Dosimetry, safety, completeness, and plan-quality agents review in real time.
  • Auto-report with source provenance Bilingual auto-generated reports with AUTO / YOU / BOT source badges.
